PBMC ageing workflow — active next steps

Completed presentation, reporting, and output-organization items have been
removed. The remaining items need further analysis, implementation, or a
scientific decision.

1. Investigate model behavior and normalization

- Investigate why the random-slope model plot shows horizontal study slopes,
  including examples where the study trends appear clearly non-horizontal.
- Diagnose the remaining DESeq2 fitting warnings and failures in gene
  profiling. Examples include overflow/invalid-value warnings for S100A10 in
  CD16 monocytes and a non-estimable study-slope design for IL16 in
  proliferating T cells. Batch profiling now continues after a failed gene;
  determine why these fits fail and make their diagnostics informative.
- Assess how zero counts and linear-model pseudocounts affect results, using
  SOX as an example. Evaluate whether capping the geometric-mean normalization
  target at 1e6 changes low-count distortion; do not apply a cap before
  understanding its effect.
- Investigate the fixed offset between naive normalized counts and VST values.
  Determine which DESeq2 quantities or filtering decisions explain the offset,
  and whether an absolute-expression trend is present.

2. Improve interpretation of gene-level results

- Determine whether per-gene linear-model estimates can be validly converted
  to a scale comparable with DESeq2 log2 fold changes. The report currently
  labels the distinct scales; do not present them as directly comparable
  without a justified conversion.
- Investigate whether the VAMP5/IFN-related signal in CD14 cells appears in
  age-bin residual correlations, and keep it visible even if those genes are
  not among the top ageing hits.

3. Extend covariate and residual analyses

- Test whether the memory CD8 CMV residual signal can be recovered using genes
  with the strongest CMV-positive versus CMV-negative difference in AIFI, and
  assess whether those genes classify or cluster other samples.
- Choose the count measure and transformation for coloring single-cell UMAPs,
  then assess whether the resulting view is informative.

4. Improve report navigation and model-result publishing

- Extend the central HTML report index with selected key results; it currently
  links to harmonization QC, integration benchmark, combined trajectory, and
  per-type reports.
- Clarify which per-gene outputs are from continuous-age, age-bin, and
  study-slope linear models, and whether a shared merged linear-model result
  artifact is needed in addition to the published focused per-gene results.

5. Reduce repeated work and extend analyses

- The per-gene workflow has an on-disk DESeq2 model cache. Consider fitting
  and publishing the shared model during differential-expression analysis,
  then loading it once in memory when generating multiple gene reports.
- Relate technology and intron-related measures to gene properties, including
  5'/3' UTR length and intron length. First specify which intron measure is
  intended.
- Extend cross-cell-type DE comparisons to include AIFI L1 class, distinguishing
  shared T-cell programs from subtype-specific programs. Account for sequencing
  depth and cell numbers when comparing types.
